WELCOME TO THE VAULT

Attention, aspiring cyber explorers!

Somewhere in this facility, our team of Master Hackers has hidden a secret vault. Only the worthy will crack the code to open it.

Mission

You have been given access to a digital terminal. On this terminal, you will see a stream of numbers. Your task is to decipher the code to open the vault.

Hints from the Master Hackers:

  • “Look back at the past to discover the future.”
  • “A sequence cybersecurity specialists love. Leonardo Bonacci...”
  • “Only the last two numbers matter for the vault. Combine them carefully.”
  • “Some say AI can be helpful… if you know how to ask.”

Rules

1. Do not brute force the vault. Think. Observe. Deduce.

2. Use the sequence and any patterns you can find.

3. The vault code is 4 digits long, made by combining the last two numbers of the sequence.

4. Use paper, pen, or your AI assistant (shhh… we won’t tell).

5. Once you think you have cracked it, you may enter your code in the vault.

Question for the Prospective Hacker

Can you crack the code? Do you have what it takes to think like our master hackers?
